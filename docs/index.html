Solar Project ROI Calculator

Enter a commercial system size and state — the calculator auto-fills the real EIA utility rate and NREL solar capacity factor, then computes IRR, NPV, simple and discounted payback, LCOE, and a 25-year cash flow stream. Federal ITC (30%) and MACRS 5-year depreciation modeled per IRA 2022 and IRS Publication 946. Generates a formatted Excel proposal report. Built for commercial solar EPC financial analysis.
