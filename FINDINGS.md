# Key Findings — Solar Project ROI Calculator

## What the Model Confirms

A standard 100kW commercial rooftop solar system in Arizona (Tucson/Phoenix area):

- **IRR: 12.7%** — above most commercial hurdle rates of 8-10%
- **NPV: $147,000** — positive at 7% WACC over 25 years
- **Simple payback: 7.3 years** — well within the 20+ year panel life
- **Lifetime savings: $644,000** — 3.3x the net project cost

These numbers use real EIA utility rates (11.66¢/kWh for Arizona) and
NREL capacity factors (19.5% for Tucson). Not assumptions pulled from air.

---

## The ITC Makes or Breaks Projects

Before ITC: $280,000 net cost. After ITC: $196,000.

That $84,000 federal tax credit (30% per IRA 2022) drops the effective cost
by 30% and adds roughly 2.5 percentage points to IRR.

Without ITC and MACRS combined, the same project drops from 12.7% to 8.1% IRR.
For many commercial clients, that difference is what puts a project above or
below their internal hurdle rate.

The ITC is locked at 30% through 2032 per the Inflation Reduction Act.
Projects breaking ground in 2033 face a step-down to 26%.

---

## Rate Escalation Is the Most Optimistic Assumption

The model assumes 2.5% annual utility rate escalation.

EIA historical average: US commercial rates have increased about 2.1%/year
over the last 20 years. 2.5% is slightly above trend — not unreasonable but
worth flagging in client conversations. At 1.5% escalation the IRR drops
to 10.8%. At 3.5% escalation it rises to 14.7%.

This is the single assumption most worth stress-testing before presenting to clients.

---

## MACRS Adds More Than Most Clients Expect

On a $196,000 net cost project (after ITC), MACRS 5-year depreciation
generates approximately $32,000 in NPV of tax benefits at 21% corporate rate.

For clients paying full corporate tax rate, this is real cash. For tax-exempt
entities (nonprofits, municipalities), MACRS provides no benefit — a fact
worth addressing early in the sales conversation.

---

## The Sensitivity That Matters Most

From the tornado chart (±20% variation in each input):

1. **Electricity rate** — ±5.6pp IRR swing
2. **Capacity factor** — ±5.6pp IRR swing
3. **System cost ($/W)** — ±5.3pp IRR swing

O&M cost is nearly irrelevant (±0.4pp). Discount rate affects NPV heavily
but barely touches IRR. The three things that determine whether a project
pencils are rate, sun, and cost — in that order.
